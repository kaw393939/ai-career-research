# The evidence behind the examples

These employer pages were inspected in **Chrome on November 5, 2026**. The library focuses on US jobs and remote roles explicitly supporting US applicants. It is a purposive sample for comparing work and eligibility, not a census of jobs or a measurement of demand.

**Open when checked** means that a description and application link or form were visible. We did not apply or verify continued hiring. **Status uncertain** includes future-opportunity pipelines. A US remote label does not guarantee sponsorship or eligibility in every state.

## Interpretation notes

- **A stage is not a universal years band.** United's senior title has a lower stated minimum than some other experienced positions. WEX's staff-level scope carries much greater experience expectations. Compare ownership and requirements together.
- **A role can be entry level and still inaccessible to a particular student.** Degree level, graduation timing, citizenship, work authorization, and location can each be decisive.
- **Compensation is selective.** T-Mobile and GM specify internship pay units clearly. Atlassian displays both substantive hourly ranges and zero-valued placeholders; its pay is not summarized in the cards. Scale's internship page contains full-time salary boilerplate, so we do not reinterpret it as an internship stipend. Other ambiguous periods are left explicit or omitted.
- **Posting evidence is narrower than occupation evidence.** These descriptions show what selected employers ask for. They cannot establish that a title is new, rapidly growing, or typical across the market.

The [methodology](../research/methodology) defines inclusion and status rules. Return to the [job explorer](./) to compare responsibilities, requirements, and editorial portfolio ideas.
