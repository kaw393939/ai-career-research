# AI Evaluation Engineer

An AI evaluation engineer builds repeatable tests that reveal capabilities, failures and the effects of system changes.

**Classification:** AI-focused specialty · **Foundations:** Experimental design, Testing · **Focus:** measurement and reliability

**Related titles:** evaluation engineer, model evaluations research engineer, evaluations research scientist

<span id="how-this-role-is-changing"></span>
<span id="what-the-work-looks-like"></span>

## What you do

Define measurable behavior, build datasets and scoring runners, investigate failure categories and regressions, and report evidence for training or release decisions. Open-ended outputs require explicit rubrics and judgment. Work ranges from application tests to frontier research; distinguish ownership of tests/infrastructure from short-term data rating.

<span id="skills-to-develop"></span>

## Skills

- Python, data analysis and software testing.
- Statistics, experimental design and measurement noise.
- Rubrics, data quality and benchmark versioning.
- Domain knowledge to recognize plausible but incorrect answers.
- Clear reporting of failures and uncertainty.

<span id="entry-routes-and-search-terms"></span>

## Example requirements

**Anthropic — Research Engineer, Post-Training Model Evaluations:** trustworthy training measurements, regression diagnosis and model-quality communication; asks for strong Python, production-system experience and careful interpretation of ambiguous results.

**Scale AI — Machine Learning Research Scientist, Evaluations:** benchmarks, failure mechanisms and training interventions; expects substantial ML/post-training knowledge, with advanced degrees and research publications **preferred**.

QA automation, software, analytics, research assistance and domain expertise offer related foundations. Research-lab roles can require deeper ML experience than application evaluation.

<span id="a-portfolio-project-that-shows-the-work"></span>

## One project

**Document-assistant evaluation suite.** Use public or synthetic documents.

1. Write **60 original questions** across answerable, ambiguous, unsupported and adversarial categories; define correctness, source support and abstention rubrics.
2. Keep a held-out split and compare two configurations under identical conditions.
3. Have **two reviewers score 20 answers** independently; report agreement and disagreements.
4. Publish category results, repeated-run variation, setup and **five investigated failures**.

Include provenance, a reproducible runner, results and a release recommendation explaining unmeasured behavior. If using a model judge, manually audit a sample and document its errors.
