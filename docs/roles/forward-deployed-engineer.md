# Forward-Deployed Engineer

A forward-deployed engineer works closely with customer teams to turn their operational problems into deployed software, increasingly including AI applications.

**Focus:** customer delivery · **Useful starting point:** software engineering and stakeholder communication · **Also search:** FDE, FDSE, forward-deployed software engineer

::: info About the name
“AI forward engineer” may refer to this role, but it is not a standardized equivalent. Search for **forward-deployed engineer** and verify the actual responsibilities. A title alone cannot establish that a position involves AI.
:::

## What the work looks like

Palantir's **Forward Deployed Software Engineer** example combines customer discovery, application development, data work, architecture decisions, and ownership through deployment. It expects engineers to communicate with both technical teams and executives. This is hands-on implementation work within that employer's platform and customer environment. [Palantir employer example](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/7ea66860-ae93-4139-9388-b7284cb33f37)

Anthropic's **Forward Deployed Engineer** example centers on building production AI applications inside customer systems, supporting deployments, and feeding reusable implementation patterns back to product teams. Its Munich posting also identifies substantial travel and language requirements. Those conditions belong to that particular position, not the whole occupation. [Anthropic employer example](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5391016008)

Our interpretation: this role suits people who enjoy moving between code, incomplete requirements, and conversations with users. Ask whether the team owns long-term maintenance, works on pilots, supports sales, or does all three; those distinctions affect daily work.

## Skills to practice

| Capability | Evidence you can show |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery | Interview notes that turn a broad complaint into a testable problem |
| Software and data | A working integration with a useful interface and documented data flow |
| AI delivery | Evaluations and explicit handling of uncertain model output |
| Communication | A short demo, decision log, and understandable status updates |
| Operational judgment | A handover guide, failure scenarios, and a rollback plan |

This is a suggested practice checklist synthesized from the examples. The particular tools depend on the employer and customer; Python, TypeScript, databases, and cloud concepts are useful areas to investigate in postings.

## Portfolio project: an embedded delivery case study

Partner with a student organization, nonprofit, or small business on a bounded workflow, such as routing incoming requests. Use synthetic or approved public data for the public demonstration.

Suggested acceptance criteria:

1. Write a one-page problem statement describing the user, constraints, and success measure.
2. Record a baseline for the original workflow; label estimates separately from measurements.
3. Deliver an application that a representative user can try from start to finish.
4. Route uncertain or consequential decisions to a human and demonstrate that path.
5. Test at least 20 realistic cases, including missing data and failed dependencies.
6. Publish a handover guide and summarize feedback from at least one user session.

If no partner is available, use a clearly labeled simulated customer. Do not present invented interviews or hypothetical business improvements as observed results. A strong case study explains compromises and unresolved failures alongside the demo.

## Entry routes and title boundaries

Software engineering, data integration, technical consulting, and implementation work can provide relevant practice. Students can look for internships and early-talent programs while building delivery evidence. Palantir's student resources explicitly discuss FDSE alongside software engineering and describe early-career pathways. Eligibility and available cohorts should always be checked on the employer's current page. [Palantir early talent](https://www.palantir.com/careers/students-and-early-talent/)

Experience varies sharply: the sampled Palantir position requests at least one year of relevant post-college experience, while the sampled Anthropic Munich position requests at least eight years. Neither establishes a universal minimum. [Palantir requirements](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/7ea66860-ae93-4139-9388-b7284cb33f37) · [Anthropic requirements](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5391016008)
