# Follow the responsibility, then the title

The additional Chrome research surfaces work around AI platforms, inference performance, agent workflows, product decisions, and responsible-AI integration. These are useful areas to investigate. A few postings cannot establish that an occupation is newly created or growing quickly.

## Four more paths to investigate

| Focus | The work behind it | Employer evidence in this sample | Student starting point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [AI platform / inference engineering](../roles/ai-platform-engineer) | Make model training and serving reliable, observable, and efficient | GM student internship; Bitdeer graduate program; WEX staff platform role; Apple inference role | Build a small serving pipeline and measure its performance |
| [AI automation / agent engineering](../roles/ai-automation-engineer) | Integrate tools, workflows, model behavior, and human review | Bitdeer graduate tracks; C3 AI graduate delivery; MANTECH experienced future-opportunity listing | Demonstrate a bounded workflow with tests and a manual fallback |
| [AI product management](../roles/ai-product-manager) | Define user value, priorities, acceptance criteria, and evaluation | Merck product-manager example requiring prior professional experience | Write and test a product brief in a team project |
| [Responsible AI / governance](../roles/responsible-ai-engineer) | Implement integrations and controls, investigate failures, and communicate risk | United senior AI-risk engineering example | Create an inspectable risk and incident case study |

The table is an editorial organization of the [employer source ledger](../jobs/sources). Portfolio starting points are our suggestions, not employer assignments or substitutes for required experience.

## What changes as responsibility grows?

| Stage | What to look for in the description | What not to assume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internship | Mentorship, a bounded project, school enrollment, dates, and academic prerequisites | That any undergraduate can apply or no prior research is required |
| Entry level | An explicit graduate or early-professional route, accepted project evidence, and graduation windows | That an entry label means any degree or any location is eligible |
| Mid level | Independent implementation, production work, customer delivery, or product ownership | That all employers use the same years threshold |
| Senior / staff | Broader systems ownership, architectural decisions, reliability, mentoring, or organizational influence | That one employer's senior title equals another's staff scope |

For example, the library contrasts a student platform internship, a graduate program with multiple engineering tracks, and experienced roles with much broader ownership. Read the full description before interpreting the word “AI” as the main qualification.

## Transfer what you already know

Backend development can connect to AI application integration. Linux, cloud, and distributed systems can connect to platform and inference work. Quantitative research and software testing can connect to evaluation. Requirements gathering and domain expertise can support product or customer delivery—but each role still has its own technical and experience requirements.

Use this as a learning hypothesis. Choose one profile, inspect its [real job examples](../jobs/), and write down the evidence you already have and one project that would close a specific gap.

## Limits of this snapshot

The examples span a small set of employers and were checked on **November 5, 2026**. They do not measure the proportion of remote jobs, typical pay, the frequency of a title, or the likelihood of being hired. Broader occupational research would need an appropriate population, geography, time period, and classification method.
